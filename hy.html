MODEL DESIGN

How the story continues

From complete shots to coherent chunks, then from learned continuation to efficient inference.

Method overview adapted from the project presentation. Diagrams are schematic.

SHOT-BY-SHOT

A single shot per target

The transition falls outside the generated unit. Independent shot encoding can also introduce audio-video timing drift.

HOLISTIC

The whole sequence at once

Attention over a long audio-video sequence brings a larger computational cost.

CHUNK-WISE

Several shots, shared context

A coherent chunk keeps complete shots together and learns how to continue the history.
