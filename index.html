THE IDEA

The story continues.
One chunk at a time.

Long videos bring a new challenge: each shot must work on its own while remaining part of the same story.

Infinity Shots learns to generate a multi-shot sequence as a series of coherent chunks. Each chunk contains complete shots and builds on the accumulated context, carrying characters, scene information, and audio forward.

TECHNICAL APPROACH

Learning the next chunk.
Remembering the story.

A progression from the data unit to the attention pattern and training history, designed around multi-shot continuation.

01 / SHOT-BY-SHOT

One shot per target

Each target contains one shot, placing the transition outside the generated unit. Independent shot encoding can also introduce audio-video timing drift.

02 / HOLISTIC

A minute is expensive

Processing the entire sequence together increases the cost of attention over long audio-video context.

03 / CHUNK-WISE

Several shots, one scene

Use complete shots as building blocks, then learn continuation at the scale of a coherent chunk.
